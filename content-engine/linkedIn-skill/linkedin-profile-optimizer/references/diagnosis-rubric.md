# Profile Diagnosis Rubric

Score each section 1–5. Share the score + specific reason when diagnosing.

## Headline (out of 5)
| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 1 | Says "student", "fresher", "looking for opportunities", or is completely blank |
| 2 | Has a job title but no differentiation or authority signal |
| 3 | Has a role + a skill cluster, but no transformation or bold claim |
| 4 | Has role + differentiator, but wording is generic or too safe |
| 5 | Instantly communicates authority, transformation, and makes you want to learn more |

## About Section (out of 5)
| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 1 | Resume copy-paste, bullet points, or completely empty |
| 2 | Has some personal writing but talks about skills not transformation |
| 3 | Mentions what they do but doesn't make reader feel the outcome |
| 4 | Has transformation angle but written in AI/corporate voice |
| 5 | Reads like a human, promises a specific outcome, and ends with a clear direction |

## Banner (out of 5)
| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 1 | Default LinkedIn blue or blank |
| 2 | Has something but it's generic (just a name or random graphic) |
| 3 | Has a message but it's vague or the design is cluttered |
| 4 | Has a clear moat statement, but design inconsistency or weak social proof |
| 5 | Single bold statement + social proof + consistent, premium design |

## Experience Titles (out of 5)
| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 1 | "Intern", "trainee", "student project" — zero authority |
| 2 | Accurate but generic (e.g., "Software Engineer at X") |
| 3 | Decent titles but no AI-native framing or modern context |
| 4 | Good titles, strong first bullet points under each role |
| 5 | Titles read like authority, AI context added where honest, first two bullets convert |

## Overall Premiumness (out of 5)
| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 1 | Immediately signals beginner/desperate — would not get a reply |
| 2 | Neutral — neither helps nor hurts, but won't stand out |
| 3 | Decent — a recruiter might read the DM, but profile doesn't help close |
| 4 | Strong — profile supports the DM and removes doubt |
| 5 | Premium — profile itself generates inbound, DM is just the trigger |

## Red Flags (auto-flag these regardless of score)
- Open to Work badge visible → tells people you need them more than they need you
- "Intern" or "Student" anywhere prominent in headline
- Last post was a meme, joke, or off-brand content
- Profile photo is dark, blurry, or clearly a selfie
- Featured section empty
- Color inconsistency across banner, photo background, and featured images
- About section over 500 words or full of bullet points
