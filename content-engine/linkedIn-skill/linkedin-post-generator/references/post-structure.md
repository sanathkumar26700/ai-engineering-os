# Post Structure Reference

## The Hook — Most Important Line You'll Write

The hook is line 1. It shows in the feed before "see more." It decides if anyone reads the rest.

### Hook Formulas That Work on LinkedIn

**The Surprising Stat:**
> "LinkedIn lowered the reach of AI-written posts by 40% this year."

**The Contrarian Opener:**
> "AI automations are not the future. They're already dead."

**The Personal Confession:**
> "I wasted 3 months fine-tuning a model that didn't need fine-tuning."

**The Bold Prediction:**
> "In 2 years, 'prompt engineer' will be as irrelevant as 'webmaster' was in 2010."

**The Direct Question:**
> "Why do most AI agents fail in production? It's not the model."

**The Specific Number:**
> "One comment got me 17,000 impressions. The post itself got 12,000."

**The Micro-Story Opener:**
> "My first ML project took 6 months. My last one took 6 hours."

**Rules for hooks:**
- Never start with "I" — start with the point
- Never start with a compliment to the reader or a philosophical observation
- If it could be the opening of a news article — it's too safe. Make it more opinionated.
- Test it by asking: "Would I stop scrolling for this?" Honestly.

**Example:**
- Refer example.md in the references folder.
- Pick a suitable example based on the topic and use similar structure

## Full Post Structure

### Format A — Opinion Post (most common, most shareable)
```
[HOOK — Bold claim or surprising angle]

[1-2 sentences of setup — what triggered this thought]

[Your core insight or opinion — 2-4 short paragraphs]

[Specific example or proof — real, not hypothetical]

[Takeaway or call to think differently]

[Optional: question to invite comments]
```

### Format B — Story Post (high engagement, humanizing)
```
[HOOK — Start mid-story or with the outcome]

[Quick context — 2-3 sentences on the situation]

[The moment — what happened, what went wrong or right]

[What you learned or realized]

[Why this applies to others in your niche]

[One line closer — punchy or thoughtful]
```

### Format C — Hot Take Post (contrarian, high risk/high reward)
```
[HOOK — The take that most people would disagree with]

[Acknowledge the popular opinion briefly — "Most people think X"]

[Your counter — why you believe Y instead]

[Evidence or reasoning — specific, not vague]

[Invite discussion — "Am I wrong? Tell me why."]
```

### Format D — Screenshot Post (easiest to start with)
```
[Attach screenshot of Reddit post, tweet, or comment as image]

[HOOK — Your reaction in one line]

[Your opinion — 2-3 short paragraphs]

[Tag or credit source if possible]

[One question to spark comments]
```

## LinkedIn Formatting Rules

**Spacing:**
- One sentence or two max per paragraph
- Always a blank line between paragraphs
- Never a wall of text

**Emojis:**
- Max 2-3 per post, only if it fits the voice
- Never use emojis as bullet substitutes (the "→" arrows are fine occasionally)

**Hashtags:**
- 3-5 max, at the very end
- Use niche ones: #AIEngineering #MachineLearning #AgenticAI #MLOps
- Not generic: #AI #Tech #Innovation (too broad, no community)

**Line breaks:**
- Use them aggressively. LinkedIn feeds are scanned, not read.
- If a paragraph is 3+ sentences, break it up.

## Timing and Frequency

- Post 5x per week. Consistency beats quality in early stages.
- Best times (IST): 8-9am, 12-1pm, 6-8pm
- Never post more than once per day
- Comment on 5+ posts the same day you publish — it signals activity to the algorithm
