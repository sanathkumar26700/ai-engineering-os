Example 1: 

One of the Quickest Productivity Hacks I've Found

*Brain Dump*

Recently, I realized that my mind was cluttered with meetings, tasks, follow-ups, notifications, and countless open loops.

The constant context switching was silently hurting my productivity.

One simple exercise helped:

Take a pen and paper.

Write down everything that's on your mind.

Don't worry about structure.
Don't organize it.
Don't write it neatly.

Just dump everything onto the page.

What you'll see on that paper is exactly what your brain has been trying to manage in the background.

Once it's out of your head, prioritization becomes much easier.

Instead of juggling 25 different thoughts, you can focus on the one thing that matters most right now.

I've found that writing things down creates mental clarity much faster than trying to "think harder" about everything.

Sometimes productivity isn't about doing more.

It's about carrying less.
