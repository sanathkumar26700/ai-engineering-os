# AI Slop Removal — LinkedIn Edition

LinkedIn's algorithm detects AI-generated content and reduces its reach. These are the exact patterns to eliminate.

---

## Absolute Banned Words (never use, ever)

If any of these appear in your post, rewrite that sentence from scratch:

- **delve** / **delve into** / **let's delve**
- **tapestry** (used figuratively — "a tapestry of insights")
- **testament to** ("a testament to your hard work")
- **landscape** (figurative — "the AI landscape", "the evolving landscape of")
- **pivotal** ("a pivotal moment", "pivotal role")
- **underscore** / **underscores the importance of**
- **foster** ("foster collaboration", "foster growth")
- **leverage** (as a verb — "leverage this opportunity")
- **synergy** / **synergize**
- **paradigm** / **paradigm shift**
- **holistic**
- **robust** (used to mean "strong" or "comprehensive")
- **seamless** / **seamlessly**
- **groundbreaking**
- **game-changing**
- **cutting-edge**
- **innovative** / **innovate** (unless quoting someone)
- **transformative**
- **best practices**
- **thought leader** / **thought leadership**
- **deep dive** (as a noun — "let's do a deep dive")
- **comprehensive** (as an adjective preamble — "a comprehensive guide to...")
- **crucial** (replace with "important" or just restructure)
- **it's worth noting**
- **it's important to note**
- **in today's world** / "in today's fast-paced world"
- **in conclusion** / "to conclude" / "in summary" / "to summarize"
- **the bottom line is**
- **let's explore**
- **I'm excited to share**
- **I'm thrilled**

## Structural AI Patterns (rewrite these)

### The Rule of Three Parallelism
AI loves listing exactly three things in matching syntax.

**AI:** "This approach is efficient, scalable, and reliable."
**Human:** "This works because it's fast. And it doesn't break at scale."

**AI:** "Whether you're a beginner, an intermediate practitioner, or an expert..."
**Human:** "Doesn't matter if you just started — this applies."

### The "Despite X... However Y" Structure
**AI:** "Despite its limitations, AI has shown remarkable progress. However, challenges remain."
**Human:** "AI is genuinely impressive right now. The limitations are real though — and people aren't talking about them."

### Significance Escalation at End of Paragraph
**AI:** "...underscoring the growing importance of agentic systems in modern enterprise contexts."
**Human:** "...which is why every ML team I talk to is asking about agents."

### The Formal Hedge
**AI:** "It's worth considering that this approach may not be suitable for all use cases."
**Human:** "This won't work for everyone — especially if your data pipeline is already a mess."

### Perfect Grammar Everywhere
Real humans make small mistakes. Fragments. Run-ons. Starting sentences with "And" or "But."

**Add one intentional imperfection per post:**
- A sentence fragment: "Which is exactly the problem."
- Starting with a conjunction: "But here's the thing."
- A casual aside: "(honestly still not sure this was the right call)"
- A colloquial shorthand: "ngl", "tbh", "rn" — sparingly, if it fits your voice

## The AI Smell Test

Before posting, read your draft aloud. Ask:

1. Could this have been written by anyone? → Bad. Make it specific to your experience.
2. Does every sentence follow perfectly from the last? → Too polished. Break the flow somewhere.
3. Is there any word a 5th grader wouldn't use? → Find a simpler word.
4. Does it end with a meaningful reflection on the broader significance of something? → Cut that. End with a human note.
5. Could you have written this in 3 minutes without thinking? → You probably did. Think more.

## Wikipedia AI Writing Signals (Source Reference)

LinkedIn is believed to use similar signals to Wikipedia's AI cleanup project. Common Wikipedia-flagged AI tells that apply to LinkedIn:

- Overuse of passive voice throughout the piece
- Sentences that explain context that doesn't need explaining ("As is well known...")
- Consistent use of em dashes (—) for dramatic pauses
- Closing with a sentence about legacy, impact, or future importance
- Mentioning "complexities" without specifying what they are
- Using "multifaceted" to avoid specifics
- Adjective clusters: "innovative, transformative, and groundbreaking"

## Quick Fix Checklist

Before finalizing any LinkedIn post, check:

- [ ] No words from the banned list
- [ ] No three-part parallel structures
- [ ] No "Despite X, however Y" pattern
- [ ] No significance escalation at paragraph end
- [ ] At least one sentence that reads like a normal human wrote it (fragment, casual word, etc.)
- [ ] Hook starts with the point, not with "I"
- [ ] Post is under 350 words
- [ ] Read it aloud — does it sound like YOU or like a press release?
