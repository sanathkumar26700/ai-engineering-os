**Track:** AI Engineering Foundations

## What this session is about

You can build a great model. Can you ship it?

F1-F3 taught you the ML. F4 teaches you everything that sits around the ML in a real job: hypothesis-driven EDA, turning data insights into business recommendations, writing production-quality code, using Git properly, and the tools real teams use to track work and communicate.

This is the session that turns a student who can build models into an engineer who can deliver projects.

---

## What you'll build

A fully packaged, documented, reproducible project — clean code structure, README, Git history, environment reproducibility, and a business recommendations slide from your EDA findings.

## The single most important idea in this session

An insight that doesn't change a decision is just an interesting fact.

The entire point of EDA, the entire point of ML, the entire point of this program — is to give someone the information they need to make a better decision than they could make without you. If your work doesn't reach that point, it's incomplete.
